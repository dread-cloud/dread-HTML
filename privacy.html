Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

This explains what personal data dread.cloud collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Who is responsible

BCR Hosting is the data controller for the data described here. Contact us at order@dread.cloud for any privacy question.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking. Fonts and images are served from our own domain. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes visitor IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from attacks.

What we collect from customers

  • Contact details: name, email address, Discord username, and your company name.
  • Billing records: invoices, payment confirmations, and transaction IDs or wallet addresses for crypto payments.
  • Service data: IP addresses, network traffic metadata, and logs we need to run the network, mitigate attacks and handle abuse.

We don't look at the contents of your servers, except in the cases set out in our Terms.

Why we use it

  • To provide and bill for services (performing our contract with you).
  • To protect our network and respond to abuse (our legitimate interests).
  • To keep records the law requires, such as tax records (legal obligation).

We don't sell personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only with providers that help us run the service: datacenter operators, network and mitigation partners, our email provider, Cloudflare, and payment processors or exchanges. We also share data when the law requires it.

Our infrastructure spans the US, the EU, Australia and Singapore, so data may be transferred between them. For data from the EU, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Account and billing records: as long as you're a customer, then as long as tax law requires, typically 7 years. Network and abuse logs: up to 90 days, unless needed for an ongoing investigation.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or object to how we use it. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. If you're in California, you have similar rights under the CCPA. Email us to make a request, and we'll reply within 30 days.

Data processing for EU customers

If you need a data processing agreement (DPA) for personal data you host on our servers, email us and we'll sign one.
